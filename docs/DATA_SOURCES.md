# Kenya data sources and evidence governance

## Candidate official sources (integration not yet performed)
| Source | Potential records | Verification requirements |
|---|---|---|
| IEBC | Official historical election results and boundaries | Check publication/version, election date and geographic units |
| Controller of Budget | Budget implementation and disbursements | Match fiscal year, vote/program and reporting period |
| Office of the Auditor-General | Audit findings and qualifications | Preserve precise finding wording; an audit query is not proof of fraud |
| National Treasury | National budgets and expenditure reports | Separate approved, revised, released and spent figures |
| PPRA / public procurement portals | Tender and contract records | Confirm award status, entity and date |
| County governments | CIDPs, ADPs, budgets and project reports | Confirm official publication and local unit |
| KNBS | Population and development statistics | Cite census/survey year and geographic methodology |
| OpenStreetMap | Basemap and selected geographic features | Respect ODbL and tile-service usage policy |

These are potential sources, not claims of current live API access, complete coverage or redistributable licensing.

## Mandatory provenance fields
Publisher, document title, original URL, retrieval timestamp, publication date, reporting period, license/usage restrictions, document hash, extracted page/section, extraction method, reviewer and verification date.

## Project verification procedure
1. Match name, agency, location and project identifiers across official records.
2. Check allocated amount versus disbursed amount versus actual expenditure.
3. Validate contract timeline and official status; reconcile contradictions.
4. Geolocate with confidence level; avoid fabricated point precision.
5. Record on-site observations as observations, not conclusive misconduct.
6. Human reviewer approves publication; publish evidence and correction pathway.
7. Revalidate on a documented schedule and retract disputed/incorrect claims.

## Integrity safeguards
Never infer that 'not found in source' means 'abandoned'. Mark unknowns explicitly. Distinguish allegation, audit finding, verified physical status and editorial analysis. Do not publish identifiable reporter details without appropriate consent and legal basis.

## Data licensing
Review each provider's terms before copying, storing, caching, rehosting or redistributing datasets. OpenStreetMap tile usage is not a production SLA.
