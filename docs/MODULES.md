## Suggested navigation
Overview / Intelligence Map / Projects / Public Finance / Elections / Citizen Reports / Research / Reports / Administration.

## Project record states
Operational: proposed → tendered → active → completed, with paused/delayed/stalled/cancelled as separately evidenced conditions. Verification: unverified → review → verified, or disputed/retracted. These are independent axes; do not confuse them.

## Delivery sequencing
Complete tenant/auth foundation and provenance before accepting live submissions. Build county/ward drilldown and projects first; finance and historic elections second; reporting, AI and advanced overlays third.
